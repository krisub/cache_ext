</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Refine the congestion phase detection threshold from 1.5x to 1.4x to more aggressively detect sustained congestion. This makes the policy more sensitive to congestion window collapse, capturing cases where CWND has dropped below SSTHRESH but may not be at the most extreme level yet. The tighter threshold (1.4x instead of 1.5x) provides earlier detection of sustained congestion phases, allowing the cache to protect working set pages more proactively when the network is in congestion avoidance mode. This complements the existing loss detection by distinguishing between transient packet loss (CWND temporarily reduced but recovering) and sustained congestion (CWND stuck well below slow-start threshold).
</DESCRIPTION>
