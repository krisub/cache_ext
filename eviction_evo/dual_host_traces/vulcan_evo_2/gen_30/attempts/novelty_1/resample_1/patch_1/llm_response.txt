sequential_vs_random_access_classification
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
This edit enhances the per-folio heat classification by incorporating global inter-arrival patterns to distinguish between sequential scans and random access. LevelDB workloads exhibit two distinct access patterns:

1. **Sequential-hot**: Index scans and sequential reads characterized by low inter-packet arrival times (< 50μs) and high access counts (> 50). These benefit from aggressive protection as they represent working-set data that will be re-accessed soon.

2. **Random-access**: Sporadic key lookups with high inter-packet arrival times (> 500μs). These are less cache-sensitive and should receive reduced protection to prevent cache pollution.

The current code applies a uniform +5000 bonus to all hot folios (interval_ewma < 100ms). This edit refines that by:
- Increasing the bonus to +6000 for sequential-hot folios (low global inter-arrival + high access count)
- Reducing the bonus to +1500 for random-access folios (high global inter-arrival)
- Keeping +5000 for intermediate cases

This asymmetric scoring improves YCSB-E performance (which has sequential scans from workload E) while maintaining stability on mixed workloads by avoiding over-protection of random-access folios that won't be re-accessed soon.
</DESCRIPTION>
