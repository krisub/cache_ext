<DESCRIPTION>
Implement asymmetric scoring based on access pattern classification to better differentiate read-heavy hot pages from mixed workload pages. This adds a tiered bonus system:

1. **Hot read-heavy folios** (access_count > 100 AND interval_ewma < 50ms): These are genuinely hot pages with frequent, rapid re-access patterns typical of YCSB-E workloads. Apply a strong +1000 bonus to protect them aggressively.

2. **Moderately hot folios** (access_count > 50 AND interval_ewma < 100ms): Apply a +500 bonus for pages with moderate frequency.

This classification helps the policy adapt to different workload characteristics:
- Read-heavy workloads (YCSB-E: 75k+ ops/sec) benefit from aggressive protection of their hot set
- Mixed workloads (YCSB-A: 4k ops/sec) don't over-protect moderately accessed pages
- Scan-heavy workloads (YCSB-F) benefit from better discrimination between working set and scan pollution

The thresholds are chosen based on observed access patterns:
- 100 accesses is a clear signal of a hot page
- 50ms interval indicates very frequent access (20+ accesses/second)
- 100ms interval indicates still-hot but less critical access
</DESCRIPTION>
