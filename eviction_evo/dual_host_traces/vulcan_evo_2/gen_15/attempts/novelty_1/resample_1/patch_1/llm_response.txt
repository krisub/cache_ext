<DESCRIPTION>
The current program's tiered access classification approach (differentiating by access_count thresholds) has hurt performance compared to Generation 5 (5099.34 vs current 4661.77). The key regression is removing the congestion-aware recency bonuses that Generation 5 had, which dynamically extended protection windows during packet loss events.

The tiered approach with hard access_count thresholds (>100, >50) is too rigid and doesn't adapt well to workload diversity. Reverting to the simpler heat-based interval classification plus restoring the congestion-aware recency logic should recover the lost performance.

This change:
1. Simplifies the heat-based scoring back to Gen 5's approach (hot < 100ms, warm < 1s)
2. Restores congestion-aware recency bonuses that increase protection by 50% during packet loss
3. Extends the protection window to 60 seconds during congestion to capture working set under stress
</DESCRIPTION>
