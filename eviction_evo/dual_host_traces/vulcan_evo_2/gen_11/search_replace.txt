<DESCRIPTION>
This edit implements congestion-aware recency decay with loss-triggered boost. The current static recency bonuses (+3000 for <5s, +1000 for <30s) are modulated based on packet loss detection. When `vulcan_get_max(GF_LOST) > 0` indicates network congestion:

1. Increase the <5s recency bonus by 50% (from +3000 to +4500)
2. Increase the <30s recency bonus by 50% (from +1000 to +1500)
3. Add a NEW third tier: extend protection window to <60s with +500 bonus

The rationale is that during congestion events (detected via packet loss), each cache miss becomes significantly more expensive due to elevated RTT and reduced throughput. By boosting recency protection during these periods, we keep the working set in cache longer when it matters most. The extended 60-second protection window catches pages that are moderately warm but would otherwise be evicted during sustained congestion episodes.

This builds on the existing RTT-aware and loss-aware protection mechanisms but adds a temporal dimension that adapts protection strength to current network conditions. The three-tier approach provides graduated protection: strongest for very recent accesses (<5s), moderate for recent accesses (<30s), and light extended protection (<60s) to handle sustained congestion periods without over-protecting cold pages during normal operation.
</DESCRIPTION>
