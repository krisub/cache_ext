<DESCRIPTION>
This change implements dual-metric sustained congestion detection to better identify when the network is experiencing prolonged congestion versus transient issues. The approach combines two reliable signals:

1. Loss persistence (lost_max > 0) - indicates ongoing packet loss
2. CWND collapse ratio - checks if the current CWND EWMA is stuck significantly below its historical minimum (< 1.3x), indicating the connection hasn't recovered from congestion

The key innovation is that this additional +1000 bonus **stacks additively** with existing bonuses (like the loss-based and congestion-phase bonuses), rather than replacing them. This provides more aggressive protection during sustained congestion events that are harder to recover from.

To support this, we need to enable both MINMAX and EWMA listeners for GF_SND_CWND so we can compare the current EWMA against the historical minimum. The conservative 1.3x threshold (vs 1.4x) ensures we detect sustained issues without false positives during normal recovery phases.
</DESCRIPTION>
