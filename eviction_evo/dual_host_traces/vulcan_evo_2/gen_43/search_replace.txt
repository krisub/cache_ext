<DESCRIPTION>
The current program regressed from generation 5 by removing jitter-aware protection and incorrectly implementing sustained congestion detection. The recommendation specified to STACK bonuses additively (+1000 when both cwnd collapse and loss persist), but the current implementation REPLACES loss bonuses by amplifying them 30%, which loses the original signal.

Key fixes:
1. Re-enable GF_MDEV_US EWMA listener (alpha=150) - this was beneficial in gen 5
2. Restore jitter-aware logic (high_jitter based on mdev_ewma > 10000) that extends protection windows
3. Fix sustained congestion to check BOTH cwnd_ewma < cwnd_min * 1.3 AND lost_max > 0 (per recommendation)
4. STACK a +1000 bonus when sustained congestion detected, rather than amplifying (replacing) existing bonuses
5. Keep the working bonuses from gen 5 unchanged, just add the sustained congestion bonus on top

This combines the best of gen 5 (jitter awareness, which scored 5667.51) with the recommendation's intent (additive stacking of sustained congestion bonus).
