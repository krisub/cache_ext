<DESCRIPTION>
Implement three-tier jitter-aware recency threshold scaling with graduated window extension. The current program uses a two-tier approach (high_jitter vs normal), but this misses the nuance of severe network unpredictability. When jitter exceeds 15ms (very high), the working set expands significantly and requires even more aggressive protection. This graduated approach provides:

1. Very high jitter (>15ms): 9-second primary window + 1400 secondary bonus
2. High jitter (>10ms): 7-second primary window + 1200 secondary bonus
3. Normal (≤10ms): 5-second primary window + 1000 secondary bonus

The incremental tuning captures increasingly expanded working sets during progressively worse network conditions, potentially adding 1-2% improvement by better adapting to severe jitter scenarios without introducing complex calculations or new metrics.
</DESCRIPTION>
