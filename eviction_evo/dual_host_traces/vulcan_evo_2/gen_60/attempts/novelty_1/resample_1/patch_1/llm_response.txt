<DESCRIPTION>
Implement graduated three-tier jitter-aware recency threshold scaling to better adapt to varying network unpredictability levels. The current program lacks jitter monitoring (GF_MDEV_US is disabled), which was shown to improve performance in earlier iterations (score 5667.51 vs current 5527.07).

This change introduces three graduated tiers based on jitter (mdev) levels:
1. Very high jitter (>15ms): Extends primary protection window to 8 seconds with +1300 secondary bonus
2. High jitter (>10ms): Uses 7 seconds primary window with +1200 secondary bonus  
3. Normal jitter (≤10ms): Baseline 5 seconds primary window with +1000 secondary bonus

The rationale is that high jitter indicates severe network unpredictability where folios are harder to predict for re-access. By graduating the response across three tiers instead of a binary threshold, we can more precisely calibrate protection levels to match actual network conditions. This captures increasingly expanded working sets during network instability while maintaining conservative protection during normal latency.

The change also re-enables and tunes EWMA listeners for optimal responsiveness:
- GF_MDEV_US: Enabled with alpha=150 to track jitter
- GF_SRTT_US: Increased alpha to 310 for more stable RTT tracking
- GF_INTER_ARRIVAL: Decreased alpha to 45 for more reactive burst detection

This graduated approach should add 1-2% improvement over the jitter-unaware baseline without introducing complex calculations that could trigger BPF verifier issues.
