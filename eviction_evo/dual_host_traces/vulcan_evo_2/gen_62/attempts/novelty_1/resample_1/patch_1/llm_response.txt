<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the binary variance check with a continuous variance-weighted regularity scoring mechanism that rewards highly predictable access patterns more aggressively. Instead of a fixed 2000-point bonus for variance < 50ms, implement a sliding scale bonus proportional to how regular the access pattern is.

The new approach:
1. Only applies to hot folios (interval_ewma < 100ms) where regularity matters most
2. Uses safe multiplication and bit-shifting instead of division to avoid BPF verifier issues
3. Computes bonus as `(5000 * (interval_ewma - variance)) >> 32`
4. This gives maximum bonus (~5000) for perfectly regular patterns (variance ≈ 0) and decreases linearly as variance approaches the mean interval

This rewards LevelDB's highly predictable sequential index scans more effectively than the current binary threshold, while maintaining BPF safety constraints. The bit shift by 32 (division by 2^32) normalizes the nanosecond-scale values to a reasonable bonus range.
</DESCRIPTION>
