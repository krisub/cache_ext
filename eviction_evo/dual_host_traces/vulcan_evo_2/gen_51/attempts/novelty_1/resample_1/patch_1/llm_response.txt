<DESCRIPTION>
The current three-tier jitter approach (8s/7s/5s with 15000/10000/0 thresholds) actually degraded performance from 5667.51 to 5401.53. The simpler two-tier approach from the previous best-performing version (5667.51) worked better. However, instead of simply reverting, I'll implement an optimized two-tier approach with slightly refined thresholds:

**Analysis of performance regression:**
- The 8-second primary window in the very-high-jitter tier is too aggressive, capturing too much cold data
- Three tiers add complexity without corresponding benefit for these workloads
- The ycsb_c_f (scan-heavy) workload particularly suffered (1824 → 1641)

**Proposed improvement:**
- Revert to proven two-tier jitter detection (single 10000 threshold)
- Use 6-second primary threshold for high jitter (down from 7s, more conservative)
- Keep 5-second primary threshold for normal latency
- Adjust secondary bonus to 1150 for high jitter (between 1000 and 1200)
- This balances working set protection during jitter with avoiding over-protection of cold pages

This approach maintains the jitter-awareness that showed benefit, but with a more conservative and proven structure that avoids the performance regression from excessive protection windows.
</DESCRIPTION>
