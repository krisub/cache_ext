access_count_scaled_cold_penalty
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The current cold folio penalty applies a uniform -100 score adjustment to all folios with interval_ewma > 10 seconds, regardless of their historical access patterns. This can over-penalize long-lived but infrequently-accessed pages that still provide value (e.g., LevelDB index structures that are accessed occasionally but are critical when needed).

This change scales the cold penalty based on access_count to distinguish between:
1. Truly unused pages (access_count < 5): full -100 penalty
2. Moderately used pages (access_count < 20): reduced -50 penalty
3. Frequently accessed historically (access_count >= 20): minimal -25 penalty

This preserves the asymmetric dual-threshold approach (hot bonus at < 1s, cold penalty at > 10s) while making the cold penalty more nuanced. The scaling prevents aggressive eviction of pages that have accumulated significant access history over their lifetime, even if their recent inter-access intervals are long. This should reduce variance in dual_small_vs_large by better protecting valuable long-lived pages while still identifying truly cold victims.
</DESCRIPTION>
