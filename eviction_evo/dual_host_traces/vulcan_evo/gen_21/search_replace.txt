access_count_scaled_cold_penalty

<DESCRIPTION>
The current program applies a uniform cold-folio penalty (-100) for all folios with low access frequency (interval_ewma > 10 seconds), regardless of their historical access count. This can over-penalize long-lived folios that have accumulated many accesses over time but happen to be infrequently accessed recently—these may be valuable index structures or lookup tables in LevelDB.

The recommendation suggests implementing **asymmetric penalty scaling**: scale the cold-folio eviction penalty inversely with the folio's access_count. Folios with higher historical access_count are demonstrably valuable to the workload, so they deserve gentler eviction pressure even if they're currently infrequent.

Proposed scaling:
- `access_count < 5`: apply full -100 penalty (truly cold/unused)
- `access_count < 20`: apply -50 penalty (moderately accessed over lifetime)
- `access_count >= 20`: apply -25 penalty (frequently accessed historically, should be preserved)

This refinement addresses the higher variance observed in the current program (dual_small_vs_large_std: 173.82 vs. best 138.46 in v5) by preventing the inadvertent eviction of moderately-valuable long-lived pages, while still aggressively evicting genuinely cold pages. The asymmetric approach aligns with LevelDB's access patterns where index blocks and metadata may be accessed infrequently but remain critical.
</DESCRIPTION>
