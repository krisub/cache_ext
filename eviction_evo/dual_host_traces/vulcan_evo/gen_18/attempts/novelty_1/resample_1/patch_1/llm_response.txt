<DESCRIPTION>
The recommendation suggests integrating PACKETS_OUT congestion signal with window saturation detection. Currently, the policy only monitors RTT and loss, but doesn't detect when the congestion window is saturated (high in-flight packets relative to cwnd).

When PACKETS_OUT EWMA approaches or exceeds SND_CWND EWMA, the network is window-limited. This is particularly valuable during the dual_congested workload where throughput is lower than the best prior version (2090.49).

The improvement:
1. Enable GF_SND_CWND EWMA listener (fast-reacting, alpha=200 to match PACKETS_OUT)
2. Compute congestion ratio: (packets_out_ewma * 100) / max(snd_cwnd_ewma, 1)
3. Apply scaled secondary boost to network_factor when ratio > 70% (indicating pressure):
   - 1.05x boost if ratio 70-90%
   - 1.15x boost if ratio > 90%

This provides finer-grained network-aware protection without the complexity of exponential decay, focusing on the immediate congestion state.
</DESCRIPTION>
