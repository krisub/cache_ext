## Key observations:
1. **dual_small_vs_large has worse p99 latency** despite being the higher-throughput scenario on that trace
2. The current scoring doesn't account for **folio size** - smaller folios (working set items) are treated the same as larger ones (bulk writes)
3. The fast zipfian reader needs its small working set protected, but large write folios can be evicted more aggressively

## Proposed improvement: Size-aware eviction bias

Add a folio-size factor to the scoring: **prefer to evict larger folios**. This aligns with the principle that small frequently-accessed folios (typical working set) have higher value-to-size ratio than large, less frequently-accessed folios.
