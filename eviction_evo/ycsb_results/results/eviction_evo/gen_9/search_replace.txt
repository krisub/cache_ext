Looking at the performance metrics across all prior programs, I can see that:

1. **ycsb_e (scan-heavy)** has significantly lower throughput (115-118 ops/sec) - this is the bottleneck
2. **uniform (random reads)** and **uniform_rw (random writes)** are also relatively weak
3. The harmonic mean is being dragged down by these weak performers

The current approach with decay and promotions is good for hot/temporal workloads but appears to be too conservative for workloads with poor locality (uniform distribution) and scan-heavy workloads.

**Key insight from the metrics:**
- ycsb_e (scan-heavy with insertions) is 118 ops/sec - pages being inserted once are staying in cache too long
- The `ACCESS_DECAY_DIVISOR` of 2 means pages only get halved every 2 seconds, which is too generous for scan workloads
- The promotion tier (tier 3) with PROMOTED_THRESHOLD of 7 might be preventing aggressive eviction of moderately-accessed pages

**Proposed improvements:**

1. **Implement frequency-recency scoring** - Instead of pure recency with binary protection rules, compute a weighted score that combines decayed access frequency and recency. Folios below a score threshold get evicted more aggressively, especially single-access pages.

2. **Aggressive single-access page eviction** - Pages accessed exactly once (common in scans) should be evicted more aggressively unless they're in the current working set.

3. **Reduce decay half-life** - Pages older than 500ms should decay faster to prevent scan pages from staying cached.

4. **Tune thresholds for better locality detection** - Lower the promoted threshold and adjust working set windows for scan-heavy workloads.

    if (is_hot && recently_used)

        return CACHE_EXT_CONTINUE_ITER;

1. **Frequency-recency scoring**: Computes `score = (effective_accesses * 10) + recency_bonus` where recency decays linearly from 100→0 over RECENT_ACCESS_NS. This creates a continuous ranking instead of binary decisions.

3. **Faster decay (500ms → 500ms)**: Stale accesses become irrelevant faster, preventing old scan pages from being protected too long.

4. **Lower promotion threshold (7→5)**: Reduces over-protection of moderately-accessed pages, allowing more flexibility for varying workload phases.

These changes should improve the harmonic mean by boosting ycsb_e (scan workload) and uniform distribution workloads while maintaining performance on hot workloads through the continued protection rules.
