<DESCRIPTION>
Implement dual-metric congestion detection by enabling MINMAX listener for GF_SND_CWND and adding a secondary congestion signal that combines CWND collapse ratio with loss persistence. When CWND EWMA drops below its historical minimum by 30% (indicating sustained window collapse) AND packet loss is present, we apply an additional +1500 protection bonus that stacks with existing loss-triggered bonuses. This provides more reliable sustained congestion identification than the CWND vs SSTHRESH comparison, which can have false positives during normal operation. The conservative 1.3x threshold avoids over-protection while the additive stacking ensures working-set protection scales appropriately with multiple congestion indicators.
</DESCRIPTION>
