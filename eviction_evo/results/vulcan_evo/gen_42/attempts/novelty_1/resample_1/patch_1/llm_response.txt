<DESCRIPTION>
The current binary variance check (variance < 50ms → +2000 bonus) doesn't differentiate between highly predictable patterns and moderately regular ones. LevelDB's sequential index scans have extremely low variance and deserve stronger protection than patterns with moderate variance.

This change implements a continuous variance-weighted regularity scoring function that:
1. Only applies to hot folios (interval_ewma < 100ms) where regularity matters most
2. Rewards lower variance more aggressively using a continuous function: `(5000 * (interval_ewma - variance)) >> 32`
3. Uses safe arithmetic (multiplication and bit shifts) to avoid BPF division constraints
4. Only applies the bonus when variance < interval_ewma (ensuring the pattern is genuinely regular relative to its mean interval)

This provides more granular differentiation of access pattern predictability, which is critical for distinguishing LevelDB's highly regular sequential scans from other access patterns with moderate regularity.
</DESCRIPTION>
