time_decay_loss_protection
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Implement explicit time-decay for loss signal protection to prevent sustained over-protection from stale loss events while maintaining responsiveness to transient congestion. 

The current approach uses RollingWindow position-based decay which relies on packet arrivals but doesn't provide precise time-based decay. This change adds explicit timestamp tracking for loss events and applies exponential time-decay where protection decays from 15% boost at loss detection to no boost after ~150ms.

This addresses the observation that Generation 39's dual_congested throughput (3696.20 MB/s) is lower than Generation 38 (3781.21 MB/s), suggesting that the graduated decay (8% and 3% boosts for old loss) is too conservative and keeps protection elevated for too long after network conditions improve.

The new approach:
1. Tracks the timestamp of the last loss event using global state
2. Applies exponential decay: full boost (115%) when loss is active, decaying linearly to no boost over 150ms
3. Provides faster recovery when network conditions improve while maintaining protection during active congestion
</DESCRIPTION>
