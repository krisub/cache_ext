<DESCRIPTION>
The current policy applies fixed aggressive bonuses during packet loss (+4500/+1500/+500) regardless of whether the network is recovering or stuck in sustained congestion. This change adds CWND recovery state detection to distinguish between brief transient loss events (where CWND is actively recovering) and sustained congestion (where CWND remains suppressed).

Rationale:
- When CWND is recovering (cwnd_ewma > min_cwnd * 1.5), the network has moved past the congestion event and doesn't require maximum protection - reduce bonuses by 25%
- When CWND is stuck near minimum, we're in sustained congestion where aggressive working-set protection is most valuable - keep full bonuses
- This adaptive approach should improve mixed workload performance (YCSB-A/B/D) by 3-5% by allowing more efficient eviction during recovery periods while maintaining protection during true congestion

Implementation:
1. Enable MINMAX listener for GF_SND_CWND to track minimum congestion window
2. Add CWND recovery detection logic in scoring function
3. Scale loss-triggered bonuses down to 75% (3375/1125/375) when recovery is detected
</DESCRIPTION>
