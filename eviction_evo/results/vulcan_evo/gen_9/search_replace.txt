add_cwnd_collapse_protection

<DESCRIPTION>
Add congestion window (CWND) collapse detection to protect the working set during severe network congestion. The current implementation monitors RTT and packet loss, but doesn't explicitly detect congestion window collapse, which is a critical indicator of severe network degradation. When the minimum observed CWND drops below 10 segments, it indicates the TCP connection has experienced severe congestion (possibly multiple fast retransmits or timeout events). During such periods, every cache miss becomes extremely expensive due to network round-trip delays.

This change:
1. Enables MINMAX listener for GF_SND_CWND (in addition to existing EWMA) to track the minimum congestion window observed
2. Adds a +2000 protective bonus to all folios when cwnd_min < 10 segments, reducing eviction pressure during severe congestion

This specifically targets write-heavy and scan-heavy workloads (like YCSB-F) where congestion window collapse correlates with poor cache performance. The protective bonus is calibrated to be meaningful (2000) but not overwhelming compared to other bonuses in the scoring function.
</DESCRIPTION>
