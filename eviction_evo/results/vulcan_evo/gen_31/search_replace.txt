<DESCRIPTION>
This edit implements CWND recovery state detection to modulate loss-triggered recency amplification based on congestion window trajectory. The current program applies fixed bonuses (+4500/+1500/+500) during packet loss regardless of whether the connection is recovering or in sustained congestion.

By tracking CWND recovery dynamics, we can distinguish between:
1. Brief loss events with quick recovery (CWND growing back) - these don't need aggressive protection
2. Sustained congestion (CWND stuck low) - these require full working-set protection

The implementation:
- Enables MINMAX listener for GF_SND_CWND to track minimum CWND values
- Detects recovery when current CWND > min CWND * 1.5 (using multiplication to avoid division: cwnd_ewma * 2 > cwnd_min * 3)
- Reduces loss-triggered bonuses by 25% during recovery (+4500→+3375, +1500→+1125, +500→+375)
- Maintains full bonuses when CWND is stuck below minimum (true sustained congestion)

This should improve mixed workload performance (YCSB-A/B/D) by 3-5% without destabilizing read-heavy workloads, as the recommendation suggests.
</DESCRIPTION>
