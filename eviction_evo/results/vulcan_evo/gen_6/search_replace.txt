<DESCRIPTION>
The current program uses two RTT thresholds (>50ms and >20ms) for network-aware protection, creating a coarse-grained scaling. By introducing a third intermediate tier at 35ms with a +37.5% bonus (calculated as 3*access_count/8), we create smoother protection scaling as network latency increases. This finer granularity should capture additional performance gains in the 20-50ms RTT range, which is common in many network configurations. The intermediate tier sits precisely between the current +50% (at >50ms) and +25% (at >20ms) thresholds, providing more nuanced adaptation to network conditions without introducing complex floating-point arithmetic or division operations that could impact BPF verifier compliance.
</DESCRIPTION>
