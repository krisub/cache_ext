loss_decay_adaptive_protection
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The current loss-based protection applies a uniform 15% boost whenever packet loss is detected (via MaxMin listener on GF_LOST), but the loss counter in kernel TCP state can persist long after network conditions improve, causing sustained over-protection that hurts throughput especially under high-frequency workloads (dual_small_vs_large). 

The proposed change implements adaptive loss protection:
1. Reduce the base loss boost from 15% to 12% to allow more eviction flexibility
2. Add additional conditions requiring BOTH packet loss AND elevated RTT to trigger boost
3. Implement decay by checking: only apply boost if max_lost > 0 AND srtt_ewma is actively high, creating natural decay as RTT normalizes
4. Combine with per-folio heat tracking: prioritize protecting genuinely hot folios even during loss events by weighting interval_ewma against the loss boost

This maintains responsiveness to transient congestion while preventing stale loss signals from locking the cache into overly conservative eviction. The improvement comes from making the loss protection conditional on BOTH persistent high RTT AND loss detection, so that as network conditions naturally recover (RTT drops), the protection automatically relaxes.
</DESCRIPTION>
