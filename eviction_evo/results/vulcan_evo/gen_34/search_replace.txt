<DESCRIPTION>
The current program regressed from 2090.49 to 1941.49 by replacing the simple cold penalty (-100) with an access_count-dependent scaled penalty. This appears to be over-protecting cold folios with moderate access counts, causing recently-active hot pages to be evicted instead.

**Root cause:** The conditional penalty logic (25/50/100 based on access_count >= 20/5/0) is too conservative and fails to prioritize true working-set pages that are both frequently accessed AND recently accessed.

**Solution:** Revert to a simple, aggressive cold penalty while strengthening recency weighting in the base score calculation itself. This combines:
1. A uniform -100 cold penalty for pages with interval_ewma > 10s (back to program 5)
2. Enhanced recency weighting in base_score using exponential-like decay (better capture of "recently-active" distinction)
3. More aggressive recency bonus (+100 instead of +50) for pages in last 3s

This targets the observation that temporal locality (recent access + frequency) is more predictive than raw historical frequency alone in LevelDB workloads. By making recency weighting dominate the base score, we prioritize recently-accessed folios regardless of total historical access count, while still maintaining simple cold-folio eviction logic.
