cwnd_aware_access_pattern_classification
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
This edit implements per-folio CWND-aware access pattern classification to distinguish sequential-hot from random-access folios. The key insight is that LevelDB's sequential index scans (common in YCSB-C, YCSB-E, YCSB-F workloads) exhibit very low inter-packet arrival times with high access counts when the network is healthy (high CWND), while random key lookups have sporadic, high inter-arrival times.

The classification applies asymmetric bonus scaling:
- Sequential-hot folios (low inter-arrival <50μs, high access count >50, good CWND >20): +6500 bonus - these benefit most from aggressive cache protection
- Random-access folios (high inter-arrival >500μs): +1500 bonus - these are less cache-sensitive and don't warrant as much protection

This should improve YCSB-C/E/F performance (scan-heavy workloads) by 2-3% while maintaining stability on mixed workloads by avoiding over-protection of random-access folios.

To support this, we also need to tune the GF_INTER_ARRIVAL EWMA alpha to be more responsive (from 100 to 60) so it can quickly detect sequential vs. random patterns.
</DESCRIPTION>
