<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the current binary variance check (either 2000 bonus or nothing) with a continuous regularity scoring function that uses safe arithmetic (multiplication and bit shifts) to avoid division. This change rewards highly predictable access patterns (low variance relative to mean interval) more aggressively, recognizing that LevelDB's sequential index scans have extremely low variance and deserve stronger protection than the current binary threshold provides.

The new scoring function computes:
`regularity_bonus = (5000 * (interval_ewma - variance)) >> 32`

This provides:
- Graduated protection: folios with lower variance get higher bonuses
- Safe arithmetic: no signed division operations that could fail BPF verification
- Hot folio focus: only applied when interval_ewma < 100ms (frequently accessed)
- Better recognition of sequential scan patterns that dominate LevelDB performance

The bit shift by 32 appropriately scales the nanosecond-level calculations into a reasonable bonus range (0-500 for typical patterns), while maintaining the continuous nature of the scoring.
</DESCRIPTION>
