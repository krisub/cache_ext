<DESCRIPTION>
Add CWND recovery state detection to modulate loss-triggered recency amplification based on congestion window trajectory. When CWND is recovering from congestion (EWMA > min * 1.5), reduce loss-triggered bonuses by 25% since the network is improving and aggressive working-set protection is less critical. When CWND remains near minimum, maintain full bonuses to protect against sustained congestion. This distinguishes brief packet loss events (which recover quickly) from sustained congestion (where working-set protection is most valuable), potentially improving mixed workload performance without destabilizing read-heavy workloads.
</DESCRIPTION>
