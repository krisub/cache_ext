<DESCRIPTION>
This edit adds per-folio CWND-aware access pattern classification to distinguish between sequential-hot folios (like LevelDB's sequential index scans) and random-access folios. The classification uses inter-packet arrival regularity from the network combined with per-folio access patterns and congestion window state.

Sequential-hot folios are identified by:
- Regular inter-packet arrival times (< 50μs EWMA) indicating sequential workload
- High access count (> 50 accesses) indicating importance
- Good network conditions (CWND > 20 segments) indicating no severe congestion

These folios receive a +6500 bonus because sequential scans benefit greatly from cache retention.

Random-access folios are identified by:
- Irregular inter-packet arrival times (> 500μs EWMA) indicating sporadic access
These receive only a +1500 bonus as they are less cache-sensitive.

This asymmetric bonus scaling (6500 vs 1500) allows the policy to aggressively protect sequential working sets (critical for YCSB-E's scan-heavy workloads) while being less protective of random one-shot accesses. The classification is placed after congestion phase detection to reuse the cwnd_ewma variable, and before recency-based scoring to provide an orthogonal protection dimension.
</DESCRIPTION>
