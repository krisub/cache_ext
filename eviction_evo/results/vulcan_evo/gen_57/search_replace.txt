<DESCRIPTION>
The current congestion window saturation detection compares EWMA of packets_out against EWMA of snd_cwnd, which introduces complexity and potential instability (division by potentially small EWMA values). The recommendation suggests a simpler approach: compare the smoothed packets_out_ewma directly against thresholds based on the raw net_snd_cwnd value.

This change:
1. Removes the division operation and percentage calculation
2. Compares packets_out_ewma directly against raw net_snd_cwnd thresholds (80% and 95%)
3. Applies more aggressive additive boosts (+15 and +25 instead of +5 and +10)
4. Uses simpler, more conservative fixed-point arithmetic

This addresses the dual_congested throughput regression by providing a more stable congestion signal that doesn't depend on EWMA of cwnd, which can be noisy or lag behind actual conditions.
</DESCRIPTION>
