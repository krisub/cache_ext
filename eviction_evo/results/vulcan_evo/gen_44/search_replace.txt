<DESCRIPTION>
The current implementation applies fixed loss-triggered bonuses (+4500/+1500/+500) whenever packet loss is detected, regardless of whether the network is actively recovering from congestion or stuck in sustained degradation. This doesn't distinguish between transient packet loss events (which TCP recovers from quickly) and prolonged congestion (where working-set protection is most valuable).

This edit extends the loss-triggered recency amplification by coupling it to CWND recovery dynamics:
- When `cwnd_ewma > cwnd_min * 1.5` (indicating CWND is recovering from congestion), reduce loss-triggered bonuses by 15% (e.g., +4500 becomes +3825)
- When CWND is stuck below minimum, keep full bonuses
- This allows the policy to be less aggressive during brief packet loss events that recover quickly, while maintaining strong protection during sustained congestion

The modulation is conservative (15% reduction vs. Generation 31's 25% that regressed) and uses a 1.5x threshold to detect recovery. This should improve performance on mixed workloads (YCSB-A/B/D currently 4028-5833 ops/sec) by 2-3% without destabilizing read-heavy workloads.
</DESCRIPTION>
