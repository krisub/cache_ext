<DESCRIPTION>
The current program regressed significantly (5667.51 → 5207.70) after adding CWND recovery detection that scales down loss bonuses to 75% during recovery. This over-conservative approach hurts performance by reducing protection too aggressively.

I'm implementing the recommended continuous variance-weighted regularity scoring using safe arithmetic (multiplication and bit shifts) to reward highly predictable access patterns. Instead of the current binary check (variance < 50ms → +2000), I'll use a continuous function that scales the bonus based on how regular the access pattern is.

The key insight is that LevelDB's sequential index scans have extremely low variance (min ≈ max) and deserve stronger protection than the current flat +2000 bonus. By computing regularity_bonus = (interval_ewma - variance) >> shift, we reward patterns where variance is much smaller than the mean interval, while maintaining BPF compliance by avoiding division.

Additionally, I'm removing the problematic CWND recovery scaling that reduced bonuses to 75%, reverting to the more successful strategy from generation 5 that protected the working set more aggressively during any network stress.
</DESCRIPTION>
