<DESCRIPTION>
Implement graduated three-tier jitter-aware recency threshold scaling as recommended. The current binary jitter detection (high_jitter > 10ms switching between 7s/5s) is replaced with three graduated tiers:

1. Very high jitter (>15ms mdev_ewma): 8s primary window, +1300 secondary bonus
2. High jitter (>10ms mdev_ewma): 7s primary window, +1200 secondary bonus
3. Normal/low jitter (≤10ms mdev_ewma): 5s primary window, +1000 secondary bonus

Rationale:
- The graduated approach captures increasingly expanded working sets during severe network unpredictability
- Prevents sudden threshold effects and over-protection during transient jitter spikes
- Conservative bonus amplification maintains protection during normal latency
- Expected to add 1-2% improvement over current jitter-aware gain without introducing complex calculations

This targets the recency protection logic which is critical for multi-workload performance where network conditions vary significantly across traces (ycsb_c_a, ycsb_b_a show high RTT variance).
</DESCRIPTION>
