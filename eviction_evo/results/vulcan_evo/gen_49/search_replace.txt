recency_normalized_access_count
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Calibrate the recency-weighted base score computation by introducing an explicit normalized access count variable. Instead of directly multiplying access_count by (100 + recency_factor) / 100, we compute a normalized_access_count that adds the recency boost as an additive component: `normalized_access_count = meta->access_count + (meta->access_count * recency_factor / 100)`. This makes the temporal locality contribution more explicit and allows network-aware protection factors to compound more effectively on top of the recency-adjusted value. The change better distinguishes between old cold folios (e.g., access_count=10, last_access=9s ago → normalized=10) and recently-active folios with similar historical counts (e.g., access_count=10, last_access=0.5s ago → normalized=15), addressing the working-set dynamics more precisely while maintaining the strong congested performance.
</DESCRIPTION>
