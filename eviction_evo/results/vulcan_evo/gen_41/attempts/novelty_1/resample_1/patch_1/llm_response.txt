<DESCRIPTION>
The current program's cwnd recovery modulation degraded performance (5201.83 vs 5667.51 from the jitter-aware program). This edit reverts the cwnd recovery logic and implements a graduated three-tier jitter-aware recency threshold system instead of the previous binary approach.

The rationale:
1. Very high jitter (>15ms): Network is extremely unpredictable, working set needs maximum protection (8s primary window, +1300 secondary bonus)
2. High jitter (>10ms): Moderate unpredictability (7s primary window, +1200 secondary bonus)  
3. Baseline (<=10ms): Normal latency conditions (5s primary window, +1000 secondary bonus)

This provides finer-grained adaptation than binary high_jitter while avoiding the failed cwnd recovery complexity. The graduated approach captures increasingly expanded working sets during network unpredictability without introducing complex calculations that might confuse the BPF verifier.
