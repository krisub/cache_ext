add_rcv_wnd_pressure_detection
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The current program detects network pressure through loss, cwnd ratio, and jitter, but does not monitor receive window (rcv_wnd) saturation. In asymmetric workloads or congested network scenarios, the receiver's advertised window can become exhausted before local congestion window is saturated. When net_rcv_wnd falls below 64KB, it indicates backpressure from the remote side, making cache misses more expensive (data can't be transmitted until receiver window recovers).

Adding this as a fifth pressure signal will catch a class of network congestion not currently detected. When rcv_wnd is low, we should apply the protective pressure multiplier (0.75x to eviction scores in the scoring approach, or extended time windows in the adaptive approach) to avoid over-evicting pages during receiver-side backpressure.

This targets the "dual_small_vs_large" trace where the slow writer (4KB values) can cause receiver backpressure, and "dual_congested" where both clients are behind proxies with limited bandwidth and may experience receive window exhaustion.
</DESCRIPTION>
