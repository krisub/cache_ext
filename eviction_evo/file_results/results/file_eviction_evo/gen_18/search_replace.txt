<DESCRIPTION>
The current program shows a regression in niceness_throughput (2313.36) compared to the best prior version (2494.39). Analysis reveals that while the graduated protection mechanism was added, it may not be aggressive enough in cleaning up moderately-accessed but stale folios from the high-priority list.

The recommendation proposes adding a **secondary demotion tier** for folios with 2-3 accesses that have aged beyond 1.5x WORKING_SET_NS. This creates a graduated demotion strategy:
- Tier 1: Single-access folios aged > 2.0x WORKING_SET_NS (existing)
- Tier 2: Moderate-access folios (2-3 accesses) aged > 1.5x WORKING_SET_NS (new)

This prevents cold pages with moderate reuse from monopolizing high-priority cache slots, freeing space for truly hot pages (6+ accesses) and improving both priority and niceness throughput. The mechanism is conservative (only demotes after 1.5x aging period) and uses integer arithmetic to avoid floating point operations.
