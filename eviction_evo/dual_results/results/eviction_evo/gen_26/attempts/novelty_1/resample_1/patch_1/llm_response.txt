<DESCRIPTION>
The current frequency bonus uses a logarithmic curve where access_count 4+ all receive 300 points, which may over-protect stale high-frequency folios that are no longer part of the active working set. This is particularly problematic during workload transitions when frequently-accessed pages from a previous phase should be evicted faster.

The recommendation proposes a steeper curve with diminishing returns above access_count=3:
- access_count 1 → 40 (down from 0, slight boost for re-accessed pages)
- access_count 2 → 90 (down from 100, reduced bonus)
- access_count 3 → 160 (down from 200, tighter threshold)
- access_count 4 → 220 (down from 300, significant reduction)
- access_count 5+ → 280 (down from 350-400, capped diminishing returns)

This preserves the multi-factor scoring architecture while improving discrimination between frequently-accessed and aged folios. The lower baseline bonuses combined with steeper diminishing returns enable faster eviction of aged high-frequency folios, which should reduce p99 latencies (currently 167-246ms) by preventing stale hot pages from blocking cache space needed by the active working set.

The change is conservative (reduces protection on high-frequency pages) but maintains enough protection to avoid thrashing on genuinely hot pages that are still actively accessed.
</DESCRIPTION>
